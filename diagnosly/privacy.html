Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

Diagnosly ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your personal health information is collected, processed, and safeguarded when using Diagnosly.

2. Data Collection & On-Device Processing

We prioritize personal privacy and data sovereignty above all else:

🔒 Privacy Guarantee: Health assessment queries and pattern recognitions are processed on-device whenever possible. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your health data.

Any telemetry or operational logs collected are strictly anonymized and used exclusively to maintain service reliability.

3. How We Use Information

Information processed by the application is utilized solely to:

  • Provide personalized preliminary health assessment insights.
  • Recognize symptom patterns and provide relevant educational health resources.
  • Continuously enhance system performance and security protections.

4. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption protocols (both in transit and at rest) to ensure that your sensitive personal data remains confidential and secure against unauthorized access.

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact our privacy team at contact@criaphore.com.
