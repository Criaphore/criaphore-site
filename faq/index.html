💳 Subscriptions & Billing

How do I unsubscribe or cancel my subscription? iOS & Android

Subscriptions are managed securely through your mobile operating system. You can cancel anytime directly via your device settings:

On iPhone or iPad (Apple App Store):
  1. Open your device Settings app.
  2. Tap your Name / Apple ID banner at the top.
  3. Tap Subscriptions.
  4. Select the app (e.g. Orbora).
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription and confirm.
On Android (Google Play Store):
  1. Open the Google Play Store app.
  2. Tap your Profile Icon in the top right corner.
  3. Tap Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions.
  4. Select the app (e.g. Orbora).
  5. Tap Cancel subscription and confirm.

⚠️ Important: Deleting or uninstalling the app from your device does not cancel your recurring subscription. You must cancel via Apple or Google settings to stop renewals. When cancelled, your Pro features remain fully accessible until the end of your current billing period.

What is RevenueCat? Security

RevenueCat is our verified in-app purchase and subscription management infrastructure partner, used by thousands of top mobile apps worldwide.

RevenueCat acts as a secure bridge between Apple's App Store, Google Play, and our backend servers to validate store purchase receipts and manage user entitlements in real time.

🔒 Zero Financial Storage: RevenueCat never receives, stores, or processes your credit card numbers or banking information. All financial transactions are managed solely by Apple or Google.

How do I restore my purchases from RevenueCat? Restoration

If you reinstalled the app, switched to a new device, or upgraded your phone, you can restore your active subscription without paying again:

  1. Make sure you are logged into the same Apple ID or Google Play account used to purchase the subscription.
  2. Open the app and log into your account.
  3. Navigate to the Account page > tap Settings > tap Restore Purchases (or tap the restore option on any paywall view).

RevenueCat will query the store, verify your active entitlement receipt, and restore your Pro access immediately.

How do I request a refund from the App Store or Google Play? Refund Policy

All in-app purchases and subscriptions are billed directly by Apple and Google. Both stores provide direct self-service options without needing to email the developer:

Apple App Store (iOS):

Apple manages all iOS transactions directly. To request a refund:

  1. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com.
  2. Sign in with your Apple ID.
  3. Select Request a refund, choose your reason, select the app, and submit.
Google Play Store (Android):

Google handles refunds directly via automated self-service:

  1. In the Google Play Store app: Tap your Profile Icon > Payments & subscriptions > Budget & order history > find the order > tap Request a refund or Report a problem.
  2. On the web: Visit Google Play Order History or use the Google Play Refund Request page, select your order, and submit your request.
How does the free trial work and when will I be charged? Billing

If you start a plan with a free trial, you receive complete Pro access for the duration of the trial with no upfront charge.

To avoid recurring charges, cancel the subscription at least 24 hours before the free trial ends in your device's store settings. If not cancelled, the trial automatically converts into your selected subscription.

Can I use my subscription across both iPhone and Android? Cross-Platform

Yes! Your Pro entitlements are linked to your registered email account. As long as you log in using the same email on both iOS and Android, your active Pro benefits sync across your devices.

🪙 Talks & Credits

Can I refund top-up credits? Credits

No. Top-up credits are consumable digital items provisioned immediately for high-performance AI generation, voice synthesis, and interactive talk sessions.

🛑 Non-Refundable: Because server computing power and AI models are allocated immediately upon acquisition, all top-up credit purchases are final and non-refundable in accordance with app store digital product regulations.

If permanently deleted, can I recover my talks? Data Policy

No. Permanently deleted talks or conversations cannot be recovered under any circumstance.

When you delete a talk or initiate account deletion, all related conversation logs, transcripts, and audio data are immediately and permanently erased from our live databases and cloud storage.

🛡️ Zero Retention Privacy: We strictly follow GDPR and CCPA privacy standards and do not retain hidden backups of deleted user conversations. Once purged, recovery is technically impossible.

Can I switch phones to get my talks on another device? Device Sync

Yes! Your talk history, profile data, and credit balance are safely linked to your registered email address, not locked to a specific physical device.

To transfer or view your talks on another phone:

  1. Install the app on your new device.
  2. Sign in with the exact same email address and method you used originally.
  3. Your talks and cloud-synced data will be restored automatically.
What is the difference between the Pro Subscription and Top-Up Credits? Usage

Pro is our single, all-inclusive subscription tier (there are no higher tiers). A Pro subscription gives you complete access to AI talk generation and comes with an included credit allowance.

  • Pro Subscription: Unlocks AI talk generation capabilities, premium soundscapes, faster cloud priority, and provides a regular monthly allowance of talk credits.
  • Top-Up Credits: Since Pro is already the highest tier, top-up packs exist strictly to generate more talks or longer talks whenever you need extra capacity beyond your monthly subscription allowance.

✨ Credits Accumulate: Credits provided by your Pro subscription are not lost at the end of the month. They roll over and accumulate in your account as long as your subscription is active!

Do credits expire or disappear at the end of the month? Credits

No. Neither top-up credits nor credits granted by your Pro subscription are lost. Unused subscription credits accumulate and stack with any top-up credits you purchase.

If you cancel your Pro subscription, any credits you have accumulated or purchased remain safely attached to your account balance for future use.

👤 Account & Data

How do I delete the app vs. delete my account? Account
1. Deleting the App:

Uninstalling the app from your phone removes local application files, but retains your cloud profile and talks for when you reinstall.

2. Permanently Deleting Your Account:

To purge your account and all associated data permanently, open the app > navigate to the Account page > tap Settings > Manage Account > tap Delete Account and confirm.

⚠️ Important Note: Deleting your account in-app does not automatically cancel an active Apple or Google subscription. You must cancel your subscription directly in the App Store or Google Play Store to prevent future charges.

Are my talks and data private? Privacy

Yes. Privacy is at the center of our architecture:

  • We never sell, broker, or monetize your personal discussions or biometric data.
  • All network communications are protected by end-to-end TLS encryption.
  • Our staff and developers do not monitor or listen to private user sessions.
Can I change my account email address? Account

No, not at this time. Your talks, accumulated credits, and profile data are permanently linked to the email address used when you first created your account.

Changing your login email is not currently supported in the app (this feature is planned for a future update). If you have lost access to your email or have an exceptional situation, please reach out to contact@criaphore.com.

🛠️ Support & Technical

Can I use the app and listen to talks offline? Connectivity

Talks in our apps are AI-generated audio sessions (there are no live conversational phone calls).

  • Generating New Talks: Generating a new talk requires an active internet connection (Wi-Fi or cellular network) because cloud AI models compose and synthesize your session in real time.
  • Listening to Talks Offline: Once a talk is generated and downloaded or saved to your device, you can listen to and replay your talks completely offline anywhere, anytime.
  • Local timers and cached soundscapes are also fully accessible without internet.
